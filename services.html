Precision Stainless Steel Services

We deliver custom fabrication, industrial OEM manufacturing, and high-durability stainless steel products engineered exactly to your requirements.

Custom Stainless Steel Water Tanks

VMR Engineers is a premier manufacturer of food-grade stainless steel water tanks. We specialize in producing custom shapes, dimensions, and capacities (ranging from 500L up to 3000L and larger) tailored specifically to fit your site conditions.

Using premium Jindal 304 and 316 grade sheets, we ensure that stored water remains 100% safe, hygienic, and free from toxic chemical leaching or microplastic contamination.

  • Jindal 304/316 Grade Sheet: Premium raw material for outstanding rust and corrosion resistance.
  • Custom Shapes & Loft Fits: Engineered vertical, horizontal, loft, or custom corner models to fit any space.
  • Safe Drinking Water: Protects stored water from heat absorption, chemical migration, and bacteria growth.

Stainless Steel Furniture Fabrication

We fabricate high-grade stainless steel furniture for residential, medical, and corporate environments. By combining modern aesthetics with the virtually indestructible nature of premium steel, we deliver functional solutions built to last a lifetime.

Our capabilities include custom designer tables, medical cleanroom benches, heavy-duty storage racks, and institutional furniture built according to strict safety and sanitation standards.

  • Medical & Lab Standards: Specialized cleanroom furniture with corrosion-proof surfaces suitable for heavy disinfection.
  • High Structural Load capacity: Heavy-gauge stainless steel storage racks and workbenches.
  • Elegant Polish & Finish: Smooth seamless welds and premium surface finishing.

Friction Accessories & OEM Custom Products

As a leading national OEM manufacturer, VMR Engineers designs and produces high-precision friction accessories and customized stainless steel components. We partner with industries to fabricate components according to exact mechanical drawings and material grades.

Our facilities utilize advanced welding, bending, and cutting technologies to execute bulk industrial commissions, custom structural brackets, and specialized hardware parts.

  • Friction Accessories: Industrial grade friction components manufactured to extreme tolerances.
  • Custom Fabrications: Bespoke brackets, steel enclosures, plates, and structural components.
  • OEM Production: Reliable manufacturing partner for bulk, high-precision industrial outputs.
